## Backend: `staff/privacy_api.py` (new endpoints), mounted directly from `staff/urls.py` (one `include` line as `path("", include("staff.privacy_api"))` placed first among the module includes), `examleaf/retention.py` (the schedule), changes in `staff/privacy.py`, `accounts/`, `pages/`, `ops/`

2. **Legal holds**: `accounts.LegalHold` (target as content type and id or a user, reason dispute | chargeback |
   claim | investigation | other, `until`, by, note, released at and by) with `GET/POST privacy/holds/`, `POST
   privacy/holds/{id}/release/` (`staff.manage_holds`, new, high: ADMIN, FINANCE, OWNER; AUDITOR reads). The erasure
   dry run (`privacy.erasure_report`) honours every hold the plan names, each as its own line with "kept until" and
   why: tax and books (invoices, credit notes and the orders behind them: 72 months after the annual return's due date
   or 8 financial years, whichever is later, computed from the financial year), a year of processing logs (audit and
   access events: kept, anonymised references only), open disputes and legal claims (LegalHold rows), the
   intermediary rule only when `SUPPORT_INTERMEDIARY_RULES` is on, the parent's confirmation for a child (an erasure of
   a minor's account waits for the parent's confirmation through the existing parent link: a `parent_confirmed_at`
   on DeletionRequest set by the link; until then the dry run says so). `DeletionRequest.complete()` obeys the same
   holds; erased accounts are never restored from a backup (the erasure ledger: DeletionRequest rows kept after
   completion with the user's keyed hash, and a management command `reapply_erasures` that re-anonymises after a
   restore; RUNBOOK gains the step). Processor tasks: on completion one inbox item per processor in the register whose
   record says it keeps personal data (`ProcessorRecord` gains `erasure_action` text and `holds_personal_data`), for
