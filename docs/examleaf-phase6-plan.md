# ExamLeaf Phase 6 — the revision LMS (app-based) and a flexible store (plan of 2026-10-08)

Founder's brief: an app-only LMS for quick revision before the exam in a short-video style (each chapter covered in 10–15 minutes with tricks, hacks and shortcuts), "all smart possibilities so that students at least pass"; references SkyLearn and django-courses (Django LMS projects) and ffmpeg; the store must have a proper admin for categories and everything else with end-to-end flexibility, with django-oscar and Saleor as references; Razorpay stays the payment gateway.

Decisions:
- The LMS is a new Django app `learn`, API-first (the mobile app is a separate project; the REST API v1 serves it; a staff-only web player exists for review). We do not adopt SkyLearn or django-courses (template-bound monoliths, not API-first, not on Django 6.1); their models are reference only.
- The store stays our own tested shop (Razorpay, COD, GST invoices, credit notes, security fixes) and grows the Oscar/Saleor ideas that matter for a publisher: category tree, product types with attributes, collections, digital products, automatic offers, staff-created orders with Razorpay payment links, order notes, a richer admin. We do not migrate to django-oscar (Django 6.1 support unverified, its dashboard and templates fight the redesign) or Saleor (a separate GraphQL service).
- Video processing runs on the existing Celery worker on a dedicated `media` queue with ffmpeg in the Docker image; outputs are HLS renditions suited to low-end Android phones (vertical 9:16: 480×854 at ~700 kbps, 720×1280 at ~1.5 Mbps, audio AAC 64 kbps) plus a poster, stored in the private storage and served through short-lived signed URLs (or the public storage when `LEARN_PUBLIC_VIDEO=1`).

## Work package D — `learn` app (revision LMS)
1. **Content model**: `Chapter` (subject from `content.Subject`, number, title, Board marks weight, previous-year frequency, "must-do" note), `Revision` (one per chapter: title, target 10–15 minutes, status draft/published, order), `Clip` (revision, order, title, kind = concept/trick/shortcut/formula/pattern/mistake/pyq, source video upload, processed HLS master path, poster, duration, transcript/notes in Markdown, free preview flag, linked `content.Question`s), `FlashCard` (chapter, front/back Markdown), `QuizItem` (chapter, question text, kind mcq/true_false/fill_blank, options, answer, explanation, source question id), tags via taggit. Admin: inline clips ordered with django-admin-sortable2 (verify Django 6.1; else simple order fields with up/down actions), processing status column, "Preview" link to the staff web player (hls.js self-hosted, CSP-safe), bulk publish.
2. **ffmpeg pipeline**: `learn/media.py` — probe (`ffprobe`), transcode to HLS renditions and master playlist, poster at 1 s, duration; Celery task on queue `media` with retries and a `Clip.processing` state machine (uploaded → processing → ready/failed with the ffmpeg stderr tail); `manage.py reprocess_clips`; Dockerfile installs ffmpeg; compose worker consumes `celery,media` (or a second worker service `media-worker`, concurrency 1); upload size limit `LEARN_MAX_UPLOAD_MB` (default 500) with the request-body limit respected (direct upload through the admin only; the app never uploads video).
3. **Imports from the production data**: `manage.py import_chapter_insights` reads `production/<subject>/format.json` (marks allotment per unit/chapter) and `production/<subject>/pyq/*.md` (what the Board asked, by chapter) to fill `Chapter` weights and frequency; `manage.py build_quiz_items` extracts one-mark items (multiple choice, true/false, fill in the blank) from the imported `content.Question`/`Solution` rows where the options and the answer parse unambiguously (report counts per subject; skip the rest).
4. **Entitlements**: `Entitlement` (user, subject or all, source = book_code/purchase/grant, valid_until), `BookCode` (unique 12-character codes printed in the books: `manage.py make_book_codes <subject> <count>` → CSV for the printer; redeem once; redemption rate-limited and logged), purchase through a digital `shop.Product` kind `digital` (no shipping; the shop builder adds the kind; `learn` listens for the paid signal and grants), staff grants in the admin. Free: the first clip of every revision and all flash cards of the first chapter (`LEARN_FREE_PREVIEW`).
5. **Progress and "pass plan"**: `Progress` (user, clip, seconds watched, completed), `QuizAttempt` (user, item, correct), chapter completion; `GET /api/v1/learn/plan/` returns a day-by-day plan from the user's exam date (`User.exam_date` nullable or request parameter), days left, minutes per day, prioritising chapters by marks weight × frequency and the student's weak chapters (wrong quiz answers), with a "minimum to pass" view: the chapters and question kinds that give the most marks per minute; `GET /api/v1/learn/revise-again/` lists flash cards and quiz items answered wrong (simple spaced repetition: 1, 3, 7 days).
6. **API v1** (`api/learn.py`, documented in API.md, OpenAPI valid): subjects → chapters (with weight, frequency, free/entitled flags) → revision → clips (signed HLS URL and poster with 10-minute expiry, transcript); progress POST; quiz items GET by chapter + attempt POST with the answer checked server-side; flash cards; plan; redeem code; entitlements; device registration `POST /api/v1/devices/` (FCM token) and a Celery beat task sending a daily revision reminder through Firebase Cloud Messaging HTTP v1 only when `FCM_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON` is set (otherwise a no-op), opt-in per user. Throttles on redeem (5/hour) and quiz attempts (600/hour).
7. **Privacy**: progress and quiz data are personal data: included in the data export, removed on deletion; no third-party video analytics; the app's device token is deleted with the account.
8. **Staff web player**: `/learn/preview/<clip>/` for staff only (hls.js from a self-hosted static file; `media-src 'self'` plus the media domain in CSP), used by content editors to check a clip after processing.
9. Tests for the pipeline (ffmpeg mocked, plus one real ffmpeg run skipped when the binary is missing), entitlement rules, plan ordering, quiz checking, code redemption limits, API permissions, export/deletion.

## Work package E — store flexibility (after the Phase 5 shop builder finishes)
1. **Catalogue**: `Category` tree (django-treebeard MP_Node as Oscar uses, verify Django 6.1; else adjacency with a cached path), products in many categories, category pages in the storefront and `GET /api/v1/categories/`; `Collection` (curated, e.g. "Board 2027 essentials") with ordering; `ProductType` with `Attribute` definitions (text, number, choice, boolean) and `AttributeValue` per product (ISBN, pages, edition year, language, class, board, subject…) shown on the product page and filterable in the API; product kinds gain `digital` (no shipping, grants a `learn` entitlement) alongside paper books and bundles; SEO fields per product (meta title/description, canonical slug history with redirects on slug change); related products.
2. **Promotions**: automatic `Offer`s (percentage or fixed amount off, on a product/category/collection/whole cart, minimum quantity or value, date range, usage caps, combinable or not) applied at cart pricing beside coupons, shown as savings lines; invoices and credit notes keep the exact discount split.
3. **Staff orders**: create an order in the admin for a phone or school order (customer, items, address, discount), send a Razorpay Payment Link (API) by email/SMS, webhook `payment_link.paid` completes it idempotently; or record an offline payment (NEFT/UPI reference) for the quotation flow; internal order notes with history; customer view in the admin (orders, addresses, entitlements per customer).
4. **Admin experience**: sortable product images (admin-sortable2), list filters and search on every store model, bulk actions (publish/unpublish, set stock, export), inline attribute values, order timeline, a store dashboard section (sales by day, top products, low stock, pending reviews, pending quotes, failed clips); import/export for products and categories; permissions through the existing roles (CONTENT_EDITOR manages catalogue and `learn` content, SALES manages orders and offers, ADMIN everything).
5. Storefront pages (category, collection, filters) use the design vocabulary; the redesign stage 2 restyles them with the rest of the shop.

## Redesign stage 2 (after A, B, E and stage 1)
Shop templates (catalogue with categories and filters, product with attributes and reviews, cart with offers, checkout stepper, order status, lookup, quotes, stock alert form), account and allauth/MFA templates (login with phone code, email code, Google, passkey; register; phone verify; My account with phone, passkeys, consent, data, orders, entitlements), emails as HTML + text on the drawn email layout, the staff web player, then a full browser pass at 375 px and 1280 px.
