# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what ExamLeaf LLP ("ExamLeaf", "we") keeps about you when you use this website or buy our books, why we keep it, for how long, and what you can ask us to do. It follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the DPDP Act).

## Who we are

ExamLeaf LLP publishes the ExamLeaf books (publisher: Bhaben Bhuyan). Registered address: [registered address]. Email: [email]. Phone: [phone]. ExamLeaf LLP decides why and how your details are used (under the DPDP Act it is the "Data Fiduciary").

## What we keep

- **When you register:** your full name, email address, password (stored only in a scrambled, one-way form that nobody can read back), class, board, district (if you give it) and date of birth.
- **For a student under 18:** the name and the phone number or email address of a parent or guardian, who gives the consent by ticking the box on the registration form.
- **A record of the consent:** what was agreed to, the version of this policy, the time, whether a parent or guardian gave it, and a scrambled (hashed) form of the internet address it came from, so that we can show that consent was given.
- **If you ask for teacher access:** your school's name, its district and the subject you teach.
- **When you use the site:** the marks, dates, times taken and notes that you choose to save in My record.
- **When you order printed books:** your name, delivery address, phone number and email address, the books ordered, the amount and the payment status. Payments are made through Razorpay; we never see or keep your card, UPI or bank details.
- **For security:** failed log-in attempts, with the internet address and browser they came from, so that we can stop password guessing. They are deleted within a day.
- **Server logs:** the pages requested, the time, the result and the internet address, to keep the site secure and to fix faults. A fixed amount is kept (50 MB for each part of the site); older entries are then overwritten, which at our traffic is after about [30] days.

We ask for nothing else.

## Why we keep it

- to let registered students open the solutions and keep their record for them;
- to confirm your email address and help you reset a forgotten password;
- to check that a teacher is a teacher;
- to deliver the books you order, issue invoices and handle cancellations and refunds;
- to keep accounts secure and the site working;
- to meet our duties under the law, for example keeping tax records of sales.

We use your details on the basis of your consent (given by a parent or guardian for a student under 18), and, for an order, to do what you asked us to do and to meet our legal duties.

## What we do not do

- No advertisements, no analytics or tracking scripts, no profiling. We do not track or monitor the behaviour of children, and we do not show them targeted advertising.
- We do not sell your details or share them with anyone for marketing.
- We use only the cookies the site needs to work: one that keeps you logged in and one that protects forms against misuse.

## Students under 18

A student under 18 can register only with the consent of a parent or guardian, given on the registration form, as the DPDP Act requires. The parent or guardian can use all the rights below on the child's behalf.

## How long we keep it

- Your account details and record: while your account exists. When you delete your account (see below) your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, district, parent or guardian details, teacher details and notes are erased after a seven-day waiting period. Only the marks and dates of your saved attempts stay, with nothing that identifies you, as statistics on the papers.
- Orders and invoices: for as long as tax and accounting law requires (currently up to eight years), even after the account is deleted.
- Failed log-in attempts: up to a day. Server logs: until they are overwritten, about [30] days (see above). Backups: [30] days, then deleted on our server and at our backup storage provider.

## Your rights

- **See your details:** on your account page, "Download my data" gives you a file with your profile, your record and your consent history.
- **Correct them:** write to us; you can change your email address yourself, and the new address must be confirmed before it is used.
- **Withdraw consent and delete your account:** on your account page, "Delete my account". The account is deleted seven days later; until then you can log in and cancel. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
- **Nominate someone** to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to: write to us.
- **Complain:** write to our Grievance Officer, [name], at [email] or [phone]. We reply within [7] days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Keeping it safe

The site is served only over an encrypted connection (https); passwords are stored only in scrambled form; staff see only what their role needs; we keep backups. If a breach of personal data happens, we will tell the people affected and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

## Changes to this policy

When we change what this policy means, we change its version (shown at the bottom of this page) and tell registered users by email before the change applies.
