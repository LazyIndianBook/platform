# Shipping Policy

This policy applies to printed books bought on this website from ExamLeaf LLP.

- **Where we deliver:** to addresses in India only. Every order needs a 6-digit PIN code and a 10-digit mobile number for the courier.
- **When we dispatch:** within [2] working days of payment, or of placing a cash-on-delivery order (Monday to Saturday, except public holidays), by [India Post or courier name].
- **How long delivery takes:** usually 5–10 working days after dispatch, sooner in Assam. Remote areas, floods, strikes and other events outside our control can cause delays.
- **Shipping charges:** a flat charge that depends on the state you ship to, free on orders above a certain value for that state. The charge and the free-shipping amount are shown at checkout, and the total before you pay.
- **Tracking:** we email you the courier and the tracking number when the parcel is dispatched; the order page shows them too, with every change of status (paid, packed, shipped, delivered).
- **Cash on delivery:** where it is offered at checkout, you pay the courier in cash when the parcel arrives.
- **Address:** please check the address and phone number before paying. If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong or nobody could receive it, we contact you; sending it again may cost a new shipping charge.
- **Damaged parcel:** if the parcel looks damaged, you may refuse it, or accept it and tell us within 7 days of delivery (see the [Refund and Cancellation Policy](/refunds/)).
