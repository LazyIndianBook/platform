**1. Answer the following as directed (any ten) :** &nbsp; `1×10=10`

(a) Write whether the relation $R=\{(a,b): a,b \in \mathbf R,\ a^2+b^2=0\}$ is reflexive. Give reason. **[1]**

(b) The number of one-one functions from a set with $3$ elements to a set with $5$ elements is ________. **(Fill in the blank)** **[1]**

(c) If $A$ is an invertible matrix and $A'$ is its transpose, then $(A')^{-1}$ is (Choose the correct answer)  **[1]**

(i) $A^{-1}$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (ii) $A$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (iii) $(A^{-1})'$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (iv) $-A^{-1}$

(d) A matrix has 2 rows and 24 elements. Write its order.  **[1]**

(e) State True or False : The function $f(x)=x^{2/3}$ is differentiable at $x=0$. **[1]**

(f) $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\log(\log x)\right)$ at $x=e$ is (Choose the correct answer) **[1]**

(i) $e$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (ii) $\dfrac1e$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (iii) $1$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (iv) $\dfrac{1}{e^2}$

(g) If $y=(1+x)(1+x^{2})(1+x^{4})$, $x>-1$, write $\dfrac{1}{y}\dfrac{dy}{dx}$. **[1]**

(h) If $f(x) = x + \dfrac1x$, $x > 0$, then $f$ is strictly decreasing on $(0, k)$ and strictly increasing on $(k, \infty)$, where $k =$ ________. (Fill in the blank) **[1]**

(i) Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{dx}{9x^{2}+4}$. **[1]**

(j) The degree of the differential equation $\left[1+\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^2\right]^3=k^2\left(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}\right)^2$ is (Choose the correct answer) **[1]**

(i) 1 &nbsp;&nbsp; (ii) 2 &nbsp;&nbsp; (iii) 3 &nbsp;&nbsp; (iv) 6

(k) State True or False : If $\hat a$ is a unit vector, then $3\hat a$ has magnitude $3$ and the same direction as $\hat a$. **[1]**

(l) If a line makes angles $90^\circ,60^\circ,30^\circ$ with the $x$-, $y$-, $z$-axes respectively, its direction cosines are (Choose the correct answer). **[1]**

(i) $0,\dfrac12,\dfrac{\sqrt3}2$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (ii) $0,\dfrac{\sqrt3}2,\dfrac12$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (iii) $\dfrac12,0,\dfrac{\sqrt3}2$ &nbsp;&nbsp; (iv) $1,\dfrac12,\dfrac{\sqrt3}2$

2. Let $f:\{1,2,3,4\}\to\{1,2,3,4\}$ be defined by $f=\{(1,4),(2,1),(3,2),(4,3)\}$. Show that $f$ is invertible and find $f^{-1}$. Also verify that $f^{-1}=f\circ f\circ f$. **[2]**

**OR**

Show that the function $f:\mathbf R \to \mathbf R$ defined by $f(x)=2x^3-1$ is one-one and onto. **[2]**

3. If $A = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 & 1 \\ 4 & 0 & -1 \\ 2 & 5 & 3 \end{bmatrix} = P + Q$, where $P$ is a symmetric matrix and $Q$ is a skew-symmetric matrix, find $Q$.  **[2]**

4. If $y=A\cos x+B\sin x$, show that $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+y=0$. **[2]**

5. If the radius of a soap bubble is increasing at the rate of $\dfrac15$ cm/s, find the rate of increase of its surface area when the radius is 6 cm. **[2]**

6. Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{dx}{\sqrt x+x}$. **[2]**

**OR**

7. Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int \sin^{-1}x\,dx$. **[2]**

8. If $G$ is the centroid of a triangle $ABC$, show that $\vec{GA}+\vec{GB}+\vec{GC}=\vec 0$. **[2]**

9. If $\hat a,\hat b$ are unit vectors inclined at an angle $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, find $|\hat a-\hat b|$. **[2]**

**OR**
