Q.4Questions

Questions parents and students ask

Are the solutions really free?

Yes. Every paper in a Sample Papers book has a QR code; scanning it opens that paper's worked solutions on this site. You can also choose the paper on its book's page.{% if solutions_require_login %} You only need to register once, with your email address.{% endif %}

Do I need the Solutions book as well?

Not to see the answers: they are free online. The Solutions book prints the worked solutions of all 30 papers, for working without a phone.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, on the order's page, until it is packed. A paid order is refunded in full to the account, card or UPI ID you paid from, within 5–7 working days. The Refunds page has the details.

I am under 18. Can I register?

Yes. Give your parent's or guardian's email address{% if sms_enabled %} or mobile number{% endif %} and we send them a link to confirm. Until they do, you can read the solutions but not save marks or order books.
