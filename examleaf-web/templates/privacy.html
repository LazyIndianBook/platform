Privacy notice

This website is run by ExamLeaf LLP. It gives students who use ExamLeaf books free access to the solutions. This notice says what we keep about you, why, and what you can ask us to do.

What we keep

  • When you register: your full name, email address, password (stored only in a scrambled, one-way form that nobody can read back), class, board, district (if you give it) and date of birth.
  • For a student under 18: the name and the phone number or email address of a parent or guardian, and the time at which the parent or guardian gave consent.
  • When you use the site: the marks, date, time taken and notes that you choose to save in My record.
  • For security: failed log-in attempts, with the internet address they came from, so that we can stop password guessing.

We ask for nothing else.

Why we keep it

To let registered students open the solutions, to keep your record for you, to confirm your email address and help you reset a forgotten password, and to protect accounts.

What we do not do

  • No advertisements, no analytics or tracking scripts, no profiling.
  • We do not sell your details or share them with anyone for marketing.
  • We use only the cookies the site needs to work: one that keeps you logged in and one that protects forms against misuse.

Who else handles it

Our hosting provider stores the site's data, and an email service delivers the verification and password-reset emails; they handle it only to run the site for us. The mathematics on the solutions pages is drawn by KaTeX, which your browser loads from the jsDelivr content network; that server sees your internet address, as with any web request, but receives none of your details.

Students under 18

A student under 18 can register only with the consent of a parent or guardian, given on the registration form, as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires. The parent or guardian can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the child's details.

How long we keep it, and your rights

We keep your details while your account exists. You may ask to see them, to correct them, or to have your account and everything in it deleted; withdrawing consent means deleting the account. Write to ExamLeaf LLP at the contact address printed on the imprint page of your ExamLeaf book.
