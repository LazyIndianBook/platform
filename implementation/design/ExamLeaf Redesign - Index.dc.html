What's still open

  • Text managed in the CMS (About, the legal pages) is shown as striped blocks.
  • Two questions on the solutions screens come from two different real papers (1(a) from E-01, 2(c) from M-04), combined to show the layout.
  • All student data (marks, clips, orders, devices) is sample data. Chapter titles are the usual Class 12 Physics chapters; the marks and question counts beside them are made up.
  • The Solutions price (₹249) is worked out from the bundle's MRP. The shop API is the source of truth.
  • Every page has a phone layout (7a and 7b). Tablet widths (600–900) aren't drawn separately: they use the phone layout with a wider column, and the desktop layout starts at 900 px.
  • The free-delivery threshold on the delivery step is illustrative. It comes from the API by state.
  • Teacher access is now drawn as the real flow: a request form (school, district, subject), then checking, then verified. Teachers can't see students yet, because the API has no link between them (G14).

Pending: not designed, waiting on a decision or the backend

  • AI answer checker. practice.AnswerSheetUpload exists, but it has no grading and is marked "planned". It isn't drawn, so it doesn't show as a working feature. It needs the product decision in docs/examleaf-ai-checker-design.md.
  • The revision course on the web (9a) is a proposal. The API is ready, but the course is only in the app. Building the web pages is a product call: same course, two places.
  • The ExamLeaf mobile app isn't in this repository, so its screens aren't covered.
  • A teacher's view of their students (G14) needs a link model and consent rules in the backend first.
  • Telling a student their parent has confirmed (G17) and claiming guest orders after registering (G16) are backend and product decisions. The screens around them are drawn.
  • Staff pages (the Django admin and the clip preview, G3) are outside the student and customer scope.
  • Other boards and classes need no new screens (board, class and subject are data), only a paper-code scheme.
